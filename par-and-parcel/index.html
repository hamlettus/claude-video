Good to know

Questions, answered.

Sizing, customisation and how the membership flexes around your golf — the things people ask before joining.

How does the customisation actually work?
Every plan includes Signature Mark: upload a logo or build a monogram in-app, and we place, size and colour-match it across your box. Want more? Full Custom (included on The Tour) pairs you with a designer to build pieces from scratch. Either way, you approve a digital proof before anything is made.
What if my sizes change?
Set your glove hand, apparel sizes and preferences once in your members' locker. Update them anytime and your next box adjusts automatically — no re-ordering, no fuss.
Can I skip, pause, or cancel?
Yes — all from your locker, anytime before a cycle ships. Skip a month, pause for the off-season, or cancel outright. There's no lock-in and no cancellation fee.
What logo formats do you accept?
PNG, SVG or PDF all work; a vector file (SVG/PDF) gives the crispest result. Our studio cleans up and colour-matches whatever you send, then returns a proof for approval before production.
How soon does my first box arrive?
Your first box ships within a week of approving your proof. After that, boxes arrive on the cadence you choose — quarterly on The Weekender, monthly on The Regular and The Tour.
Do you ship outside the UK?
Delivery is free across the UK on every plan, and we ship worldwide with rates calculated at checkout. Sizing and customisation work exactly the same wherever you play.
Can you handle team, event or corporate kit?
Absolutely. The Tour tier includes team and event kit at cost, in any quantity — society days, corporate outings or a full club roster. Tell us what you need and we'll build it.
